What does WHITESPACE do?

Every spy tool says copy the ad that is already winning, so everyone piles into the same lane. WHITESPACE does the opposite: it maps which angles are saturated in your market and surfaces the adjacent angle almost nobody is running yet — a ranked hypothesis to test, with a sourced evidence trail.
