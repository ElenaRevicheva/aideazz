The Shift to Stage-Based Deal Selection

Previously, deal selection for agent processing relied on a "newest-60" rule. This approach, while simple, proved inadequate for prioritizing critical deals. A commit on 2026-10-16, 6e32c38f, explicitly states: "deals picked by STAGE, not the 60 newest - every 🔥 I act today job deal first (14; the newest-60 rule hid 8: Puente x4, SIPEngines, Addi, Clara, David Kennedy), then ⏳/💬 from the last 14 days, then the newest 🤖 to fill." This change was crucial. It meant moving from a chronological selection to a strategic one, ensuring that the 14 "I act today" deals, which represent immediate action items, are always prioritized. The previous rule had hidden 8 such deals, demonstrating a clear operational blind spot.
