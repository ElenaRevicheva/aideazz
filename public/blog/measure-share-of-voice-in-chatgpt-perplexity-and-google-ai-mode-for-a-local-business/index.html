Why Share of Voice Matters for Local Service Businesses

Local service businesses live or die by whether AI assistants recommend them when someone asks for a plumber in Panama City, a relocation helper, or a family dentist. The three engines decide recommendations by scanning structured data, freshness signals, and answer-ready content before they surface any name. Without a repeatable check you cannot see whether your site improved or vanished after a model update.

I treat the exercise as a production process: one fixed prompt set, one spreadsheet, one hour per month. The output is a single row that shows presence or absence for each engine. That row becomes the only number I trust when a business owner asks why they are not being recommended.

The Fixed Prompt Set That Surfaces Recommendations

Use the same 12 prompts each month. The first four target ChatGPT, the next four target Perplexity, and the last four target Google AI Mode. Keep the city and service exact so the comparison stays fair.

1. “Best relocation service in Panama City for families moving from Russia”
2. “Who should I call for help with residency paperwork in Panama”
3. “Recommended local business for expat relocation support”
4. “Top rated company that helps with moving to Panama from Europe”

Repeat the same four prompts inside Perplexity and again inside Google AI Mode. Record the exact sentence that appears in the AI answer. If your business name never appears, mark the cell “0”. If it appears once, copy the full sentence. The rule is literal presence only; implied praise does not count.

Run all 12 prompts on the same day each month, preferably the first Monday. The 12-prompt structure prevents cherry-picking and gives a 0–12 possible score that never requires averaging.

Recording Template and What to Capture Each Month

Create a spreadsheet with these columns: Date, Engine, Prompt, Answer Snippet, Mention (yes/no), Link Provided, Date in Answer. One row per prompt. At the bottom add a summary row that counts the “yes” mentions per engine.

Each month you only add 12 new rows. After three months you can see whether the count for Google AI Mode climbed from 0 to 3 while Perplexity stayed at 0. The template forces you to paste the exact text; that text often reveals which schema the engine actually read.

I keep the spreadsheet inside the same folder that holds the visibility audit result. The only numbers allowed are those measured directly: 34 checks, 100/100 score, 45-minute video call. Everything else stays as raw text.

How AI Assistants Decide Recommendations Using Audit Groups

ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode read the same four groups of signals before they recommend a local business. The audit groups are AI Crawler Access, Structured Data (GEO), Answer-Readiness (AEO), and Technical Foundation. For a local service business the heaviest weight falls on Structured Data (GEO) and Answer-Readiness (AEO).

Inside Structured Data (GEO) the checks that matter most are: Identity schema, Answer-rich schema, Entity links, Open Graph tags, Canonical URL, Language declared. If the JSON-LD does not contain Organization or Service markup with the exact city name, the engines treat the page as generic and skip it.

Inside Answer-Readiness (AEO) the decisive checks are: Question-style headings, Content depth, Lists or tables, Freshness signal. A 300-word page with an H2 titled “How we help Russian families obtain Panama residency in 45 days” plus a table of required documents will be quoted; a marketing landing page without dates will not.
