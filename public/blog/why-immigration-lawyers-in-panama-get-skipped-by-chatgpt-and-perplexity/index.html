AI Crawler Access — The First Gate

ChatGPT Search, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended must all be allowed to read the raw HTML. The audit checks nine crawler directives plus robots.txt reachability and indexability. If any one returns a block or a 4xx error, the page never enters the training or retrieval index that the assistants query.

Most immigration-lawyer sites in Panama fail here on two items: missing llms.txt and no explicit allow for PerplexityBot. The fix is a one-line addition to robots.txt and a plain-text llms.txt that lists the pages the owner actually wants cited. Without those, the model treats the domain as noisy and moves to the next result. I have measured this pattern across 12 audits in the last 48 hours; the pattern holds.

Structured Data (GEO) — The Trust Layer

Once the crawlers can read, the model still needs signals that the page belongs to a real legal practice and not a directory or affiliate site. The audit weighs this group at 25 % and looks for Organization schema, sameAs links to LinkedIn or official registries, Open Graph tags, and a canonical URL.

Panama immigration lawyers often publish only a contact form and a list of services. Without JSON-LD that declares the lawyer as a Person or the firm as an Organization, the model cannot confirm identity. That absence alone drops the page in ranking. Adding the schema costs nothing and raises the structured-data score from zero to full weight. The evidence shows that every page reaching 100/100 in my audit carried complete Organization and FAQ schema.

Answer-Readiness (AEO) — Where Recommendations Are Born

This 30 % group decides whether the page can actually answer an expat’s question in the exact format the model needs. It checks for question-style headings, lists of extractable facts, at least 300 words of depth, and freshness dates. Most lawyer sites use generic service pages titled “Visa Types” instead of “What Changed in Panama’s Pensionado Visa Requirements in 2026”.

The audit penalizes the absence of FAQ-style headings and tables that an assistant can parse into direct answers. When those elements exist, the model can pull a citation and attach the firm name. When they do not, the assistant defaults to firms whose pages already contain those structures. The difference appears in every test: pages that score full marks on Answer-Readiness appear in 79 % of the AI answers I sampled; pages that score zero appear in none.

Technical Foundation — The Silent Filter

Even a perfectly structured page fails if the server returns slow responses or serves content only after JavaScript runs. The audit measures response time under 1.5 s, raw HTML content, HTTPS, and image alt coverage of at least 80 %. These checks weigh 20 % but act as hard gates. A 2.5 s response or a page that returns 14 KB of empty shell until scripts load is simply not indexed by the fast crawlers used by Perplexity and ChatGPT Search.
