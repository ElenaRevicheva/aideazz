Customs nobody explains

  • Costa (coast), Sierra (highlands), Oriente (Amazon) - very different cultures and climates.
  • Colonial charm, UNESCO heritage, arts scene, known as most polite city.
  • Try cuy (guinea pig), locro de papa, encebollado (coast).
  • Some shops close 1-3 PM.
  • Cuenca is 2,500m - take it easy first days.
  • Cuenca has 'eternal spring' - sweater weather year-round.
