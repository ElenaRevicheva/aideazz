EspaLuz on Instagram — Privacy & Data Deletion

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Run by Elena Revicheva (AIdeazz), Panama

EspaLuz is an AI companion that helps families live their new country in Spanish. On Instagram (@elena_revicheva), an automatic EspaLuz assistant does three things: it answers a comment that says "HOLA" on a post that invites it, it answers direct messages, and it answers the "start a chat" buttons. EspaLuz is an AI, not a person. This page covers only the Instagram assistant; the full EspaLuz policy is at app.espaluz.lat/privacy-policy.

1. What we keep

  • For a comment: the comment's ID and the post's ID, so the same comment is never answered twice.
  • For a direct message: your Instagram-scoped user ID (a number Instagram gives our app, not your username or phone), the last 12 messages of the conversation (each cut to 300 characters) so the reply makes sense, and how many replies were sent to you today (at most 12 a day).

We do not keep photos, voice notes or your profile details, and we do not use your messages to train AI models, sell them, or show advertising.

2. Who processes it

  • Meta (Instagram) delivers your comment or message to us and delivers our reply.
  • AI models that write the reply: Google (Gemini), Groq, xAI (Grok), OpenAI and Anthropic (Claude), one at a time; each only processes the text to write that answer.
  • Hosting: Oracle Cloud, where the assistant runs and the data above is stored.

The links in a reply (WhatsApp, the app, Telegram) take you to EspaLuz itself; what happens there is covered by the full EspaLuz policy.

3. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep it while the assistant runs. To delete what the Instagram assistant keeps about you, email aipa@aideazz.xyz with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram username. We delete it within 30 days and tell you when it is done. Removing the EspaLuz app in Instagram (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites) also stops it from receiving anything new.
