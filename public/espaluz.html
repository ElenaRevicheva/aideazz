What can my child do with Pere and Titi?

🎤 Talk

One big button. Your child speaks in Spanish or English, and Pere and Titi listen and answer in their own voices.

✏️ Trace

On a tablet, trace letters, numbers 1–10 and short sentences like “Te quiero / I love you” with a finger or a pen, then say them out loud.

📷 Show a photo

The camera opens straight away. A toy, a drawing or lunch becomes the lesson, and the heroes talk about it.

🧠 Remember

Sentences your child says come back in later lessons, so new words grow from her own words.

Questions parents ask

Is EspaLuz for children or for adults?

Both. The kids classroom with Pere and Titi is for young children. Parents and other family members learn in chat with text, voice messages and photos.

Does it work on WhatsApp and Telegram?

Yes. You can learn on the web, on WhatsApp and on Telegram. The kids lessons with Pere and Titi also run in WhatsApp voice notes.

Are photos stored?

No. A photo is described in one short sentence for the lesson, and the image itself is not kept.

Who made EspaLuz?

EspaLuz is a product of AIdeazz AI Lab in Panama, led by Elena Revicheva. She built it out of her own family's experience arriving in Panama. It isn't a generic chatbot: every feature came from a specific need the family lived at home.
